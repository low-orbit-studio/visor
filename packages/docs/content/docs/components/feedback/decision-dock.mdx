The dock is the one place an answer is given. It sits sticky at the bottom of the view, so the work above
keeps the full width, and it says exactly what will be sent. Put it last inside the page's scrolling
column.

## Kinds

- `approve`: **Needs Work** (`N`) and **Approve** (`A`).
- `pick`: **Needs Work** and **Send X** (`↵`), disabled with no key until `choice` names an option.
- `run`: **Needs Work** and **I Ran It** (`R`).

Keys typed into a field, or with ⌘, Ctrl or Alt held, are left alone. Mount one dock per page: its keys
are the page's keys.

## Installation

```bash
npx visor add decision-dock
```

This copies two files into your project, and adds Button, Kbd and Composer if they are missing:

- `components/ui/decision-dock/decision-dock.tsx` — the component
- `components/ui/decision-dock/decision-dock.module.css` — the bar, its three states and the keyframes

## Needs Work

**Needs Work** (or `N`) opens a note in place of the buttons, focused. ⌘↵ sends it (so does ↵; ⇧↵ starts
a new line), and Esc goes back. `onAnswer` gets `"Needs work"` and the note.

## Sent State

An answer turns the bar into a status line: a check drawn in the success colour, the verdict
("Approved.", "Sent B.", "Ran it.", "Needs work.") and `sentText`. A bar runs along the bottom edge for
`--decision-dock-advance` (1.2s) and calls `onAdvance` when it fills. Show the next item by changing the
dock's `key`.

Under reduced motion the dock, the note and the check appear without moving; the progress bar still runs,
since it is the countdown to the next item.

## On a Phone

Below 640px the dock spans the screen edge to edge with square bottom corners, the question wraps, the two
buttons share the row and the key hints hide.

## Glass

Over a photo (`data-glass="on"`) the dock reads the glass layer's fill, lift, blur, shadow and ink. Elsewhere
it is the card surface at 82% with a 22px frost, so the page reads through without its text competing.

## Customization

| Property | Default | What it sets |
|---|---|---|
| `--decision-dock-bg` | the glass fill, or the card surface at 82% | The bar's fill |
| `--decision-dock-filter` | the glass blur, or `blur(22px) saturate(150%)` | The frost behind the bar |
| `--decision-dock-radius` | `var(--radius-2xl)` | Corner radius |
| `--decision-dock-inset` | `var(--spacing-4)` | Gap to the bottom of the view |
| `--decision-dock-max-width` | `65rem` | The bar's widest measure |
| `--decision-dock-advance` | `1.2s` | How long the sent state's bar runs |
