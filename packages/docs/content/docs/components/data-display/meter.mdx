Meter draws use against a limit: hours against a contract's ceiling, a month against its included hours. The track is the owner's hue tint, the used fill is that hue's ink, a tick marks the heads-up point, and a label sits under it. `hue` reads Paper's project hues, so switch the site theme to **Paper** (light or dark) to see the colours as designed. On other themes the meter falls back to neutrals.

## Row meter

The compact size is one meter per row. Pass the heads-up point as a mark with `threshold`. Once `value` reaches it, the label's `<strong>` figure turns weight 800 in ink, never the alert colour (Client B below is past 80%).

## Contract meter

`size="contract"` is the full meter for one engagement. `zones` draw bands behind the fill (solid up to the low, hatched up to the high) and the pale track runs to the ceiling. Marks with a `label` and `caption` write text under their tick. `hideWhenNarrow` drops a mark's text under 30rem while keeping its tick, so a legend beside the meter still explains it.

## Past the ceiling

Use past `max` is unresolved, so by default (`overflow="alert"`) it stripes coral, the one alert case. The track stretches 6% past `value` to make room, and the fill stops at the limit.

## Retainer months

A retainer's overage is billable, not a problem: `overflow="ink"` stripes it in ink and runs the fill through it. Give every month the same `scale` so the included-hours tick lines up.

## Colour

`hue` takes a Paper project-hue angle (75, 99, 123 … 339) and sets three variables on the meter: `--meter-hue-fill` (the contract track), `--meter-hue-fill-strong` (the row track and the zones) and `--meter-hue-ink` (the fill). For a colour that is not a Paper hue, leave `hue` off and set them yourself:

```tsx
<Meter value={30} max={40} style={{ '--meter-hue-fill': '…', '--meter-hue-fill-strong': '…', '--meter-hue-ink': '…' } as React.CSSProperties} />
```

`--meter-tick`, `--meter-alert` and `--meter-hatch-width` override the tick colour, the overflow colour and the stripe width. Every part carries a `data-slot` (`meter-track`, `meter-fill`, `meter-zone`, `meter-overflow`, `meter-tick`, `meter-marks`, `meter-label`) for anything else, such as dimming the fill of an ended contract.

## Accessibility

- The track has `role="meter"` with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and `aria-valuenow`. Its name comes from the label slot, or from `aria-label` when there is no label.
- `aria-valuenow` never exceeds `aria-valuemax`; say how far past the limit the use went in the label ("12 h past the ceiling").
- Ticks, zones and stripes are decoration inside the meter. Mark text is ordinary text.
- Past the threshold the label changes weight, not only colour, and the overflow stripes have a pattern as well as a colour (WCAG 1.4.1).
- The label is 13px on Paper in the secondary text colour, which meets AA on the page and card surfaces.
