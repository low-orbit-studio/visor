Add LayerChart, a zoomable icicle, and SpanStrip, its day span strip. `LayerChart` takes a tree (`data`), a `measure` accessor (zero leaves the chart; a parent with no value sums its children), a `focusId` or `defaultFocusId` and `onFocusChange(id, node, reason)`. Rows are layers: the focus's ancestors as compressed path rows, the focus row, then three layers below it at falling heights, with a gutter naming each layer. A click (or `Enter`) opens a block to full width and its children open below it, animated over 480ms (none under reduced motion); `Esc` goes up a layer and `←` `→` move along the main row. Fills take one Paper project hue per top-level node (`hue`, or `"tide"` for the studio's investment), lighter by depth, and a `kind`: `solid`, `covered` (pale with a dot grid), `not-billable` (hatched, the label on a plate), `unresolved` (coral, the only coral), plus `today` (paler). Labels step down by width, children under 6px fold into one "+n" block at least 24px wide, and every block is a button named with its value. `renderBlock` is a slot at the foot of wide blocks (an estimate Meter), `renderCard` the hover card, and `band` an optional band under the rows where it is measured, with a note where it is not. `SpanStrip` draws a day's spans as 5-minute cells in lanes (attended and calls; agent in its own lane) on a time axis, with a legend of totals. New `layer-chart` component tokens (`investment-*`, `alert-fill`, `alert-text`, `duration`, `easing`, `ground`); Paper binds the investment hue to Tide and the unresolved fill to coral mixed into the page. `npx visor add layer-chart`.
