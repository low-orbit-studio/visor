Spinner glyph slot (VI-860): a theme sets `components.spinner.glyph` to a mask image (a `data:` URI) and the ring Spinner draws that glyph in place of its border ring, inked with `glyph-color` (default tone) or `primary-edge-color` (primary tone). A theme that leaves it unset renders exactly as before.
