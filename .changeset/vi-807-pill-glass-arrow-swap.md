VI-807: Button gets the Honcho kit's pill. New `components.button` hooks (`lg-height`, `lg-padding`, `default-shadow`, `default-shadow-hover`, `outline-text`, `outline-bg-hover`, `hover-scale`, `press-scale`, `press-transform`, `press-duration`, `motion-duration`, `motion-easing`) add the kit's height, lift, grow and press; unbound, none changes a pixel. A `glass` variant is a solid ringed control on a plain page and reads the glass layer's control fill, ring, ink and blur under `data-glass="on"`. `ArrowSwap`, exported beside `Button`, is two arrows in a one-arrow window that slide on the button's hover or keyboard focus.
