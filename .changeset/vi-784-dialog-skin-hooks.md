Dialog takes theme hooks for its panel and page scrim: `--dialog-max-width`, `--dialog-radius`, `--dialog-bg`, `--dialog-padding`, `--dialog-shadow`, `--dialog-backdrop-filter`, `--dialog-overlay-bg` and `--dialog-overlay-blur`, bindable under `components.dialog`. Unbound, Dialog and ConfirmDialog render as before in both densities.
